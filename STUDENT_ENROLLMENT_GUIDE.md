# Student Enrollment Guide

## How to Enroll in Courses

After the removal of automatic payment processing, we now offer a personalized enrollment experience where our team works directly with each student to ensure the best learning path.

## Enrollment Process

### Step 1: Browse Courses
- Visit our course catalog at the homepage
- Browse courses by category or search for specific topics
- Click on any course to view detailed information

### Step 2: Review Course Details
Each course page provides:
- Complete course curriculum and modules
- Instructor information and background
- Course requirements and prerequisites
- Contact information for enrollment

### Step 3: Contact Our Enrollment Team

You have several ways to get in touch:

#### Email
- **Address**: enrollment@yourschool.com
- **Response Time**: Within 24 hours
- **Best For**: Detailed questions, multiple course inquiries

#### Phone
- **Number**: (123) 456-7890
- **Hours**: Monday - Friday, 9 AM - 5 PM EST
- **Best For**: Quick questions, immediate assistance

#### Online Inquiry Form
- Available on every course page and our contact page
- Pre-fills with course information
- Include specific questions or requirements

### Step 4: Enrollment Consultation
Our enrollment team will:
1. Discuss your learning goals and background
2. Recommend the best courses for your needs
3. Explain pricing options and payment plans
4. Answer any questions about the course content
5. Provide information about prerequisites or recommended preparation

### Step 5: Complete Enrollment
Once you've decided to enroll:
1. Complete enrollment paperwork (provided by our team)
2. Arrange payment through your preferred method:
   - Bank transfer
   - Check
   - Credit card (manual processing)
   - Payment plans available
3. Receive your course access credentials within 24 hours

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How long does the enrollment process take?
**A**: Most enrollments are completed within 24-48 hours after your initial contact. Complex cases or payment arrangements may take longer.

### Q: Can I enroll in multiple courses?
**A**: Yes! Our enrollment team can help you plan a learning path across multiple courses and may offer bundle pricing.

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?
**A**: We accept bank transfers, checks, credit cards (manual processing), and offer payment plans for qualifying students.

### Q: Do you offer refunds?
**A**: Yes, we have a satisfaction guarantee policy. Details will be provided during enrollment.

### Q: Can I get a discount for multiple courses?
**A**: We offer various pricing options and may provide discounts for multiple course enrollments. Discuss this with our enrollment team.

### Q: What if I'm not sure which course is right for me?
**A**: Our enrollment team specializes in helping students choose the right courses. Schedule a consultation to discuss your goals and get personalized recommendations.
